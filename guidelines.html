Submission Guidelines

Author's Guidelines

Authors are invited to submit original, high-quality, and previously unpublished research articles. Submissions must not be under review by any other publication or conference. All manuscripts should be submitted electronically via Microsoft CMT in strict adherence to the IEEE standard two-column conference template.

Paper Formatting Guidelines

  1. Paper Size: US Letter (8.5" × 11").
  2. Paper Length: Maximum of 6 pages. Additional pages (up to 2) are permitted at ₹1000/$50 per page.
  3. Formatting: Double-column layout, 10pt Times New Roman font.
  4. Margins: Top: 1" (title page), 0.75" elsewhere. L/R/B: 0.75".
  5. File Format: PDF (Max 5MB) with all fonts embedded.
  6. No page numbering should be included.
  7. Use the official IEEE MS Word template for formatting.

General Instructions

  1. Papers violating formatting guidelines may be rejected.
  2. Submissions must be original, unpublished work not under review elsewhere.
  3. Simultaneous submissions are strictly prohibited.
  4. All manuscripts must be written in English.
  5. The base page limit is six (6) pages.
  6. All figures, tables, and references must fit within the page limit.
  7. Papers will be reviewed based on originality, significance, and clarity.
  8. Final decisions rest with the Technical Program Committee.
  9. If paper registered and submitted fee successfully but not meeting quality critaria will be rejected.
IEEE Similarity and Plagiarism Policies

Guidelines for Maintaining Academic Integrity

We advise authors to submit their paper by taking into account the following conference policy on plagiarism:

  • The overall similarity rate of a conference paper submission be less than 15% including reference material.
  • The overall similarity rate of a conference paper submission be less than 10% excluding reference material.
  • The similarity rate for a single source should not be more than 2% if any.
  • The AI-generated text must be strictly 0% (Not *%).
